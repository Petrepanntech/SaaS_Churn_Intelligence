Strategic Recommendations Playbook

Targeted interventions directly addressing the 3 primary churn drivers

Pillar 1: Structural Contract Shift Eliminate 4.27x Odds

Targeted Annual Agreement Incentive (15% Rebate)

With Month-to-Month accounts driving $5.1M (65%) of annualized revenue leakage, offer a 15% discount for converting to 1-year commitments. Transitioning 20% of M2M contracts secures over $450k in annual ARR.

Pillar 2: Rapid Technical Triage 48-Hour SLA

Deploy Solutions Engineers for Ticket Surges (≥4)

Accounts logging 4+ support tickets exhibit a 44.2% churn rate (2.70x odds). These represent integration blockers rather than churn intent. Dispatch a senior solution engineer within 48 hours.

Pillar 3: Dormancy Early Warning Day 7 Warning Trigger

Pre-empt the 14-Day Inactivity Disengagement Cliff

Crossing 14 days of inactivity spikes churn to 43.4%. Automate customer success playbooks to ping department champions at Day 7 to reassign idle seats before disengagement becomes permanent.
