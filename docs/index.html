Empirical Findings & Actionable Business Insights

Statistical conclusions derived from relational audit and predictive churn modeling across 5,000 accounts.

Empirical Finding 01

4.27x Churn Odds on Month-to-Month Contracts

Month-to-month contracts experience a 31.8% churn rate compared to 9.9% for 2-year agreements (Odds Ratio: 4.27x). Locking accounts into multi-year agreements represents the single highest-leverage retention driver.

Empirical Finding 02

Support Ticket Velocity is an Early Warning Signal

Accounts opening 4 or more support tickets in a 30-day window exhibit a 44.2% churn rate vs 22.6% for low-friction accounts (Odds Ratio: 2.70x). High ticket volume reflects integration friction rather than healthy product engagement.

Empirical Finding 03

14-Day Inactivity Threshold Triggers Churn Cascade

Accounts with zero user logins for 14 or more consecutive days show a 43.4% churn rate (Odds Ratio: 2.53x). Automated re-engagement campaigns must trigger at day 7 to prevent dormant accounts from entering the irreversible drop-off zone.
